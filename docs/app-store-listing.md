# App Store listing, version 1.8.34

The description and What's New sent to App Store Connect for version 1.8.34 (en-US), through nomac's update_store (a PATCH naming only these fields). The review notes are in app-store-review-notes.md. What's New covers everything since the live 1.7.9.

## Description

Talon is a fast, native chat client for Urbit. Sign in with your ship and get the full daily-use chat loop: direct messages, group channels, threads, and reactions, with your messages going straight between your device and your own ship.

Your ship is your server. Talon connects your device directly to the Urbit ship you control, so your messages, your contacts, and your history stay yours. No accounts to create, no middlemen, no analytics, no tracking.

FEATURES

• Direct messages, group DMs, and group channels
• Threads, reactions, replies, edits, and deletes
• Notebook and gallery channels
• Voice and video calls
• Party lines: drop-in group voice and video rooms
• Voice messages and dictation
• Image, video, and file attachments
• Link previews, location and calendar tags
• Calendar and tasks
• Mail, when your ship runs a mail app
• Search, bookmarks, activity, and watchwords
• Group admin tools: roles, kicks, bans, and channel permissions
• QR code sign-in
• Multiple ships, one tap to switch
• Delivery indicators
• Per-chat mute and folders
• Dark, light, and system themes
• Optional AI, off by default: catch-up summaries, an assistant, and Orrery

OPTIONAL AI

Nothing goes to any AI service until your ship has Armillary credit. Armillary is an app on your own ship that gets model access for you, so there are no keys to copy in, and Talon asks for model providers that keep nothing.

Armillary credit is a separate, optional paid service, sold by the vendor ship you choose. Talon's developer runs the default one. That vendor sees your ship's name, your balance and your purchases, and in proxy mode your AI requests pass through its ship on the way to the model provider.

Talon requires an Urbit ship to sign in. New to Urbit? Learn more and get a ship at urbit.org.
